Welcome home

Divine Grace, a community for every generation.

We are a mixed-age congregation rooted in the Lutheran tradition and gathered around worship, Scripture, prayer, fellowship and compassionate service.

Whether you have been part of our church for years or are visiting for the first time, there is a place for you here. Come as you are, meet our family, and discover the joy of walking together in faith.
